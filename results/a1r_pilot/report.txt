COST OF FREEZING Z   (realised geometric mean of alpha_eff)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Z is frozen so that the gate stays a fixed function of the
observation.  The price is that the realised budget drifts as
training moves the state distribution.  That drift is not a bug to
be corrected online -- correcting it would reintroduce the moving
scale the freeze exists to prevent -- but it MUST be reported,
because a realised budget away from alpha means A1r is partly an
'A1 at a different alpha' comparison.

  sigma factor is sqrt of the alpha ratio, since sigma* propto

  If 'spread' collapses towards 0 the allocation has flattened and
  A1r has quietly become A1.

  Whatever slope appears here is background correlation between the sigma
  head and the geometry.  A1r's tau is read against THIS number, not zero:
      tau = (A1r slope - +0.0282) / (1 - +0.0282)
